# LorrySystem document-based catalog and ICP reference

Source: Lorry_System_Solutions_and_ICPs.docx, dated 6 October 2026. The seven ICPs are proposed marketing profiles, not confirmed market qualification facts.

## Solution lines

- AI dashcams and driver safety monitoring: ADAS road safety alerts; DMS fatigue and distraction monitoring; two-channel and four-channel camera configurations; incident photos and videos. Intended problems: Unsafe driving, limited incident evidence, disputed accident claims, and difficulty monitoring driver behaviour. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- GPS tracking and fleet telematics: Vehicle location, journey history, overspeed alerts, driving event monitoring, and fleet visibility through web and mobile platforms. Intended problems: Limited visibility of vehicle movements, unauthorised use, and difficulty supervising distributed fleets. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Live vehicle and cargo monitoring: Live streaming, playback, intercom, and additional cameras for side, rear, or cargo visibility, depending on configuration. Intended problems: Cargo security concerns, delivery disputes, and difficulty verifying events during transit. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Transport Management System (TMS): Quotation, order management, trip assignment, driver and operator apps, electronic proof of delivery, invoicing, dashboards, and master data. Intended problems: Disconnected transport processes, manual dispatching, missing delivery documents, and delayed billing. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Consolidation and multi-drop delivery management: Collection-to-warehouse workflows, consolidation planning, regional delivery assignment, multi-drop jobs, and end-to-end booking traceability. Intended problems: Difficulty tracking shipments across collection, warehouse handling, and final delivery. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Fleet and driver administration: Vehicle checklists, attendance, incident records, maintenance tracking, permit and inspection records, driver incentives, and trip expenses, according to project scope. Intended problems: Fragmented records, missed renewals, inconsistent inspections, and poor visibility of operating costs. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Plant and weighbridge logistics digitalisation: Custom workflows covering orders, truck arrival, queues, loading, weighing, dockets, delivery status, and customer notifications. Intended problems: Heavy reliance on WhatsApp, repeated data entry, loading errors, and limited visibility across plant operations. Custom implementation, subject to project requirements and scope; not a confirmed standard deployment-ready product. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Waste collection and transport workflow systems: Custom customer portals, orders, credit-check workflows, manifests, fleet records, driver incentives, expenses, and dashboards. Intended problems: Disconnected collection records, manual manifest handling, and limited coordination between customers and operations. Custom implementation, subject to project requirements and scope; not a confirmed standard deployment-ready product. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.
- Custom logistics workflow automation and integration: Customer portals, operational dashboards, automated notifications, and interfaces with existing systems, subject to requirements. Intended problems: Repetitive communication, duplicate data entry, and operational information spread across multiple systems. Custom implementation, subject to project requirements and scope; not a confirmed standard deployment-ready product. Do not promise guaranteed safety, savings or improvements; establish the existing system and specific unmet gap first.

## Proposed customer segments

- Transport and haulage operators: Malaysian companies operating trucks, prime movers, trailers, or delivery fleets; owners and managers actively involved in operations. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Accident exposure, driver behaviour, disputed claims, fleet visibility, and manual dispatching. Best-fit solutions: AI dashcams, telematics, TMS, fleet administration. Buying roles: Owner, Managing Director, Operations Manager, Fleet Manager, Safety Manager.
- Third-party logistics providers (3PLs): Companies coordinating transport, warehousing, subcontractors, consolidation, and multiple customers. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Shipment traceability, subcontractor coordination, delivery updates, e-POD, and billing accuracy. Best-fit solutions: TMS, consolidation management, customer portals, telematics. Buying roles: Managing Director, COO, Head of Transport, Logistics Manager, IT Manager.
- Manufacturers, distributors, and retailers: Businesses moving goods through their own vehicles, contracted transporters, or a mixed fleet. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Delivery visibility, transporter accountability, customer complaints, and missing delivery evidence. Best-fit solutions: TMS, customer portals, e-POD, telematics and cameras for owned or authorised vehicles. Buying roles: Supply Chain Director, Logistics Manager, Distribution Manager, Operations Director.
- Premix, quarry, concrete, and construction-material operators: Plants managing frequent truck movements, weighing, loading, and site deliveries. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Truck queues, order changes, wrong loads, weighbridge coordination, and WhatsApp-based communication. Best-fit solutions: Plant logistics systems, weighbridge integration, scheduling, dashboards, notifications. Buying roles: Owner, General Manager, Plant Manager, Planning Manager, Logistics Manager.
- Waste collection and environmental-service companies: Operators managing customer collections, transport fleets, manifests, and recurring service jobs. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Collection scheduling, manifest traceability, fleet availability, expenses, and customer reporting. Best-fit solutions: Waste transport workflow systems, fleet administration, portals, telematics. Buying roles: Managing Director, Operations Manager, Fleet Manager, Compliance Manager, Finance Manager.
- Bus and passenger transport operators: Operators of public, charter, school, employee, or tourist buses. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Driver fatigue, passenger safety, incident investigation, and fleet supervision. Best-fit solutions: AI driver monitoring, multi-camera MDVR, GPS tracking. Buying roles: Owner, Operations Director, Fleet Manager, Safety Manager.
- High-value or sensitive cargo transporters: Fleets carrying electronics, valuable goods, or cargo requiring close supervision. Relevant needs to investigate (not assumed): Cargo loss, tampering, disputed handovers, and customer assurance. Best-fit solutions: Cargo cameras, live monitoring, GPS, e-POD. Buying roles: Managing Director, Transport Manager, Security Manager, Logistics Manager.
