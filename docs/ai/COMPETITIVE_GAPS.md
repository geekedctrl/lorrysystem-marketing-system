# Competitive gap audit — 10 October 2026

RakanSales advertises unified WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram/live chat/email messaging and customer history: https://rakansales.com/
Its features advertise lead scoring, Kanban pipelines, forms, forecasts, bulk campaigns, routing and customizable triggers: https://rakansales.com/features/
AI automation advertises business knowledge, qualification, follow-ups and human takeover: https://rakansales.com/features/ai-sales-automation/

These are public advertised capabilities; implementation, isolation, AI quality, reliability, API contracts, pricing and tier availability are unknown. Do not copy assets or trade dress.

LorrySystem's immediate differentiator is evidence-linked prospect research and product relevance → campaign strategy → human review. Existing pipeline, workspaces and controlled email are reused. Unified inbox, conversational AI, assignments, official channel connectors and conversion attribution are later milestones. Public advertising does not establish equivalent prospect-specific research strategies or hard budget enforcement.
