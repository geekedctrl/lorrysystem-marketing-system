# Product workspace roadmap

Updated: 6 October 2026.

The customer needs to manage several distinct products in one app. Each product has its own team and restricted access. A product workspace is the access and data boundary; individual offerings such as TMS and GPS can remain catalog entries inside LorrySystem's workspace.

The original September roadmap deferred additional product lines to MVP3. The workspace foundation has now been implemented earlier because it affects every automation stage. Read [current status](../status/current-status.md) rather than treating the original phase placement as an outstanding requirement.

| Stage | Intent | Remaining focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MVP1 | Prove the controlled discovery, research, qualification, matching, draft, approval and outreach loop on the existing self-hosted environment. | Validate real research/profile results and adapt the later workflows to workspace context. |
| MVP2 | Expand operations and infrastructure after the basic loop is stable. | Revisit cloud migration, additional channels, operating controls and provider integration against actual needs. |
| MVP3 | Scale mature automation, analytics and product operations. | Richer campaign coordination, analytics and cross-product administration without weakening team boundaries. |

Workspace credentials, catalog/ICP context, discovery memory and sender identities must remain product-specific. Multi-product campaign scheduling, richer campaign analytics and enforced provider budgets are not completed by the workspace foundation alone.

Use the [LorrySystem ICP](lorrysystem-icp.md) only for that product. New products need their own reviewed ICP and catalog. The [original commercial roadmap](../archive/planning/2026-09-17-original-mvp-roadmap.md) retains the original fees, budget proposals and phase assumptions; this implementation update does not renegotiate those terms.
