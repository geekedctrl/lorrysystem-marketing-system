**ICP = Ideal Customer Profile**

# ICP 1 — Logistics, Haulage & Commercial Transport Operators

## Typical Companies

- Logistics companies
- Haulage companies
- Freight operators
- Transport companies
- Trucking companies
- Delivery fleets
- Distribution companies
- Companies operating commercial transport fleets

## Geography

Initial focus:

- Malaysia

Future expansion can include other markets.

## Fleet Size

Fleet size should be treated as a **lead-scoring factor**, not a strict requirement.

| Fleet Size       | Priority               |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1–5 vehicles    | Potential lead         |
| 6–20 vehicles   | Good opportunity       |
| 21–100 vehicles | Strong opportunity     |
| 100+ vehicles    | Enterprise opportunity |

## Typical Decision Makers

- Owner / Founder
- Managing Director
- Operations Manager
- Fleet Manager
- Transport Manager
- Safety Manager
- Procurement Manager

## Common Pain Points

- Poor visibility of vehicle locations
- Manual transport operations
- Too much paperwork
- Manual proof-of-delivery processes
- Customers repeatedly asking for delivery status
- Driver behaviour problems
- Driver fatigue or distraction
- Accident disputes
- Lack of video evidence
- Fuel theft or excessive fuel usage
- Poor fleet utilisation
- Difficulty managing drivers and vehicles
- Vehicle maintenance tracking
- Security concerns

## Relevant LorrySystem Solutions

- Transport Management System (TMS)
- GPS / Fleet Tracking
- AI Dashcam
- ADAS
- Driver Monitoring System (DMS)
- Fuel Monitoring
- e-POD
- Fleet Maintenance Features
- Additional telematics integrations

- Lack of video evidence

- Vehicle tracking
- Driver identification
- Fleet safety compliance
- Security monitoring

## Relevant LorrySystem Solutions

- MDVR / Multi-Camera Video Monitoring
- AI Dashcam
- Driver Monitoring System (DMS)
- ADAS

- Video Telematics
- Fleet Monitoring

Messaging for this ICP should focus heavily on:

**Safety + Driver Monitoring + Passenger Protection + Video Evidence**

rather than primarily focusing on logistics workflow features such as e-POD.

# Shared Qualification Criteria

Regardless of ICP category, the Lead Generation Agent should try to identify:

1. **Company name**
2. **Industry**
3. **Location**
4. **Approximate fleet size**
5. **Type of vehicles**
6. **Relevant decision maker**
7. **Business pain points**
8. **Recent fleet expansion or activity**
9. **Current technology / fleet-management solution if known**
10. **Relevant LorrySystem product**
11. **Potential buying signal**
12. **Lead score**

---

# Lead Generation Principle

The system should **not** simply find companies with vehicles and send generic messages.

The intended workflow is:

```text
Discover Company
        ↓
Identify ICP
        ↓
Research Company
        ↓
Understand Fleet
        ↓
Detect Pain Points / Buying Signals
        ↓
Match LorrySystem Solution
        ↓
Identify Decision Maker
        ↓
Calculate Lead Score
        ↓
Generate Personalised Outreach
```
